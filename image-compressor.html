Why Use Client-Side Image Compression?

Traditional online compressors upload your private photos to external third-party servers to process them. This introduces significant privacy concerns, slow upload times for large images, and limits on file sizes.

ToolForge solves this by running the entire compression and resizing pipeline locally inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and native WebAssembly encoders. Nothing is uploaded, providing instantaneous results and complete privacy.

How GST is Calculated: Formulas & Slabs

1

Adding GST (Exclusive)

Formula: GST Amount = (Original Cost × GST%) ÷ 100.
Net invoice = Original Cost + GST Amount.

2

Removing GST (Inclusive)

Formula: GST Amount = Cost - [Cost × {100 ÷ (100 + GST%)}].
This extracts the true base cost from an MRP sticker price.

3

CGST, SGST & IGST

Within the same state, tax is split 50/50 between Central and State. Across state boundaries, the entire tax is filed as Integrated GST (IGST).

Why Client-Side PDF Processing Protects Your Data

Traditional PDF utilities upload your documents to cloud servers to perform merging operations. When working with sensitive contracts, medical records, invoices, or bank statements, uploading files to unfamiliar servers poses grave confidentiality risks.

ToolForge uses pdf-lib compiled to run entirely inside your browser's JavaScript engine. Your documents never traverse the internet, are never stored in a temporary cloud folder, and remain 100% private on your own machine.

Best Practices for Printing & Scanning

  • Maintain Contrast: Always ensure the foreground color is significantly darker than the background. Light QR patterns on dark backgrounds can confuse older barcode scanners.
  • Adequate Quiet Zone: Keep at least a 2-to-4 module border around your QR code so scanner cameras can reliably detect the bounding perimeter.
  • Vector for Print: For physical flyers, billboards, packaging, or restaurant menus, download the vector SVG format so your printer can scale it to any size without pixelation.

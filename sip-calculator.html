How Compound Interest Powers SIP Wealth Growth

1

Rupee/Dollar Cost Averaging

By investing a fixed monthly sum, you automatically buy more units when markets are low and fewer units when markets are high, lowering your average cost.

2

Exponential Compounding Curve

In early years, returns look modest. After 7 to 10 years, compounding kicks into overdrive, causing annual returns to surpass total annual contributions.

3

Discipline Over Timing

Attempting to time market highs and lows consistently underperforms systematic monthly compounding over 15 to 20 year horizons.

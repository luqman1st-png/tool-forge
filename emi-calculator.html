The EMI Calculation Formula

The standard mathematical formula used by global banking institutions and financial calculators is:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n - 1]

Where:

  • P = Principal loan amount borrowed.
  • r = Monthly interest rate (Annual interest rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100).
  • n = Loan duration in months (Years × 12).

Loan Reduction Tips

  • • Make extra payments: Even one extra EMI per year can shave multiple years off a 30-year mortgage.
  • • Choose shorter tenure: While monthly payments are higher, total lifetime interest is cut in half.
  • • Shop around for rates: A 0.5% difference in interest saves tens of thousands on major loans.
